DEPARTMENT OF FOOD & PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION (DFPD)
STATE CIVIL SUPPLIES CORPORATION LIMITED
SCHEDULE OF TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS - WELFARE GRAIN FORTIFICATION

Item Description: Procurement and Supply of Fortified Rice Kernels (FRK) for Blending in Targeted Public Distribution System (TPDS) and PM-POSHAN Welfare Schemes.

1. Product Definition & Process:
   - Commodity: Fortified Rice Kernels (FRK) produced via hot extrusion technology.
   - Raw Material: Clean broken rice grains milled into rice flour and blended with micronutrient premix.
   - Blending Intent: FRK shall be homogeneously blended with milled raw/parboiled rice at a 1:100 ratio (1 kg FRK per 100 kg conventional rice).

2. Technical & Micronutrient Specifications:
   a. Iron (as Ferric Pyrophosphate / Sodium Feredetate): 280 to 425 mg per 100g of FRK.
   b. Folic Acid: 750 to 1250 micrograms per 100g of FRK.
   c. Vitamin B12 (Cyanocobalamin): 7.5 to 12.5 micrograms per 100g of FRK.
   d. Moisture Content: Shall not exceed 12.0 percent by mass.
   e. Grain Dimensions: Length between 5.0 mm and 7.5 mm; true density between 1.30 and 1.50 g/cm3.

3. Statutory Compliance & Quality Mandate:
   - The manufacturing unit must possess a valid Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) License for Standard Mark (ISI Mark).
   - Reference Standards: In strict accordance with Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution guidelines and Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) Fortification Regulations.
