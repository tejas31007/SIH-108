STATE ELECTRICITY TRANSMISSION CORPORATION LIMITED
TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION SCHEDULE - ELECTRICAL DISTRIBUTION WIRING

Item Name: Low Tension Power and Control Flexible Cables for Substation Control Room.

1. Scope:
   Supply of single-core and multi-core PVC insulated copper conductor flexible cables.
2. Technical Specifications:
   a. Operating Voltage: Suitable for working voltages up to and including 1100 Volts AC, 50 Hz.
   b. Conductor: High conductivity plain electrolytic copper conductor, flexible Class 5 stranded.
   c. Insulation: Special Flame Retardant (FR) PVC insulation compound Type A.
   d. Sheath: Flame Retardant Low Smoke and Halogen compound, extruded outer sheath.
   e. Temperature Rating: Max continuous operating temperature 70 deg Celsius.
   f. Voltage Test: Withstand 3.0 kV AC rms test voltage in water for 5 minutes without breakdown.
   g. Preferred Brands: Material shall strictly be sourced from Havells or Polycab manufacturer catalogue.
