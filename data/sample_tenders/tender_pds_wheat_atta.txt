FOOD & CIVIL SUPPLIES DEPARTMENT
GOVERNMENT CONSUMER COOPERATIVE STORES FEDERATION
NOTICE INVITING TENDER - SUPPLY OF PACKAGED WHOLE WHEAT FLOUR (ATTA)

Subject: Procurement of 25,000 Metric Tonnes of Milled Whole Wheat Atta for Public Distribution System (PDS) Fair Price Shops.

1. Technical Specification Schedule:
   a. Product: Whole wheat atta obtained from sound, clean milling quality wheat grains, free from rodent contamination, insect infestation, or artificial coloring.
   b. Chemical Parameters:
      - Moisture: Not more than 13.0% by mass.
      - Total Ash (on dry basis): Not more than 2.0%.
      - Acid Insoluble Ash: Not more than 0.15% on dry basis.
      - Gluten (dry basis): Minimum 7.0%.
      - Alcoholic Acidity: Not more than 0.18%.
   c. Packing: Poly-laminated 10 kg consumer pouches with tamper-evident seal.
   d. Sourcing Condition: Material shall strictly be sourced from Aashirvaad Atta or Fortune Atta brand packaging.
