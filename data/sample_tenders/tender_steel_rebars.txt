MUNICIPAL CORPORATION ENGINEERING DEPARTMENT
NOTICE INVITING TENDER (NIT) - SCHEDULE OF TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Item Description: Supply and Delivery of Thermo-Mechanically Treated (TMT) Steel Reinforcement Bars for Flyover Construction Project.

1. Technical Requirements:
   a. Material & Grade: High strength deformed steel rebar Grade Fe 500D with high ductility.
   b. Nominal Sizes: 12 mm, 16 mm, 20 mm, and 25 mm diameter in standard 12-meter lengths.
   c. Mechanical Properties:
      - 0.2% Proof Stress / Yield Stress: Minimum 500.0 N/mm2 (MPa).
      - Tensile Strength (Rm): Minimum 565.0 N/mm2.
      - Ratio of Tensile Strength to Yield Stress (Rm/Re): Not less than 1.10.
      - Elongation at gauge length: Minimum 16.0%.
   d. Chemical Composition:
      - Carbon: Max 0.25%
      - Sulphur: Max 0.040%
      - Phosphorus: Max 0.040%
   e. Testing: Bend and Rebend test shall be conducted on selected samples from each 50-tonne heat.
   f. Reference Standard: Conforming to IS 1139 specification.
