ITEM CODE: CPWD-DSR-2024-5.22.6 | TENDER SPECIFICATION ANNEXURE “A”

Supply and delivery of High Strength Deformed Steel Bars (TMT) grade Fe 500D for Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) work in substructure and superstructure of Flyover Span Pier P4-P12 conforming to IS 1786 : 2008 (Fourth Revision with Amendments 1 to 3).

Mandatory Statutory Mark: All supplied steel bars must bear valid, legible Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) ISI Certification Mark under Scheme-I of Schedule-II in strict compliance with the Steel and Steel Products (Quality Control) Order, 2024.

Mechanical & Physical Parameters: Minimum 0.2% Proof Stress ≥ 500.0 N/mm²; Tensile Strength ≥ 565.0 N/mm² (Tensile/Yield Ratio ≥ 1.10); Percentage Elongation ≥ 16.0%; Total Elongation at Maximum Force ≥ 5.0%. Chemical composition shall strictly satisfy IS 1786 Table 1 limits (Carbon max 0.25%, Sulphur max 0.040%, Phosphorus max 0.040%, Carbon Equivalent max 0.42%).

Verification & Test Mandate: Manufacturer’s Test Certificate (MTC) directly traceable to batch heat numbers shall accompany every consignment. Random 100cm test specimens shall undergo bend test (IS 1599) and rebend test (Clause 9.4) at an NABL-accredited BIS-recognized laboratory prior to staging. Any manufacturer meeting standard requirements shall be eligible.

Official GeM Technical Schedule Annexure Form No: GFR-144I-VER-26108
Technical Specification Annexure for Tender Invitation Rule 144(i) Neutral

Commodity: Thermo-Mechanically Treated (TMT) Carbon Steel Bars for Concrete Reinforcement.

Applicable Indian Standard: IS 1786 : 2008 (High Strength Deformed Steel Bars and Wires for Concrete Reinforcement — Specification).

Grade Specification: Fe 500D (Ductile grade for seismic resilience). All material shall possess valid BIS Certification Licence (CM/L number) and carry the official ISI mark as per Quality Control Orders issued by the Ministry of Steel.

Mechanical Specifications: Proof Stress: 500 MPa min; Tensile/Proof Stress Ratio: 1.10 min; Elongation: 16% min; Mandrel Rebend Test: Cl 9.4 conforming.

Certified as Competition-Neutral and fully compliant with Rule 144(i) of General Financial Rules, 2017.
